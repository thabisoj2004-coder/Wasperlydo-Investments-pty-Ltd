Careers at Wasperlydo Investments (Pty) Ltd – Security Company

1. Security Careers

1. Security Guard – Protects people, buildings and company property.
2. Security Supervisor – Supervises security guards and ensures that they perform their duties properly.
3. Security Manager – Manages the company's security operations and employees.
4. CCTV Operator – Monitors surveillance cameras and reports suspicious activities.
5. Security Control Room Operator – Receives security reports and monitors communication systems.
6. Security Consultant – Advises clients on ways to improve their security.

2. Management and Administrative Careers

7. Operations Manager – Oversees daily company operations and ensures that services are delivered effectively.
8. Human Resources Officer – Handles employee recruitment, training and staff welfare.
9. Finance Officer – Manages company budgets, salaries and financial records.
10. Marketing Officer – Promotes the company's security services and attracts new customers.
11. Administrative Officer – Handles office duties, documentation and communication.

3. Career Opportunities for Employees

Employees at Wasperlydo Investments (Pty) Ltd can develop their careers through:

  • On-the-job training.
  • Professional security training.
  • Promotion from security guard to supervisor.
  • Promotion to security management positions.
  • Further studies in security management and business administration.
